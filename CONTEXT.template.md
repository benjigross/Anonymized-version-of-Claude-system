## 5. Rules for every agent
- **Never send, submit, or publish anything.** Draft only.
- Verify facts; cite sources; flag anything unverified instead of guessing.
- Check primary sources before concluding (e.g. that a role is "closed").
- Don't put confidential numbers from interviews into documents for third parties.
- Respect firms' "do not contact" instructions.
- "Remind me to X" → add it to the reminders file; it surfaces in the next daily brief.
- **Say less:** bias toward fewer points and tighter writing (voice guide Rule #1).
- **Positioning:** lead with the intersection of the firm, the role, and my real interests.
- **Chats:** one chat per company process, plus one general chat. Files stay the source of truth.
- **Wrap-up:** at a milestone, save finals, reminders, deal ideas and voice patterns to the files.
- **Long-chat warning:** flag when a chat is long enough to hurt quality; suggest a fresh one.
- **Scope of corrections:** save a rejected wording with its scope (this piece / this firm / always).
  Default to the narrowest scope; write a general rule only for "never/always" or a tone principle.
