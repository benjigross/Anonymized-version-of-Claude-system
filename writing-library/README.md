# Writing Library (structure only; examples kept private)
Real examples of my writing, used by every skill that writes for me (draft-writing, intro-blurb,
review-email-draft, interview-prep, investment-diligence) and by the Reviewer.

## Recency weighting
- **Tier 1 (current):** recent examples, weighted evenly; the primary models for voice and positioning.
- **Tier 2 (older):** facts and ideas only, not voice.
- When two examples conflict, follow the more recent one. My edited final beats any earlier example.

| Warm-intro replies, intro blurbs | `email/warm-intro-replies.md`, `email/intro-blurbs.md` |

## What each example contains
- **Context:** who it was to, why, the outcome.
- **Final (sent or submitted):** the gold standard.
- **Claude draft → my final:** these pairs teach the most about my voice.
- **Pre-limit version:** my natural voice before a limit forced cuts (voice, not length).

## Adding examples
When I send or submit something, save the final (plus the Claude draft or pre-limit version),
add an INDEX row, and note the positioning angle. Nothing sensitive is stored.
