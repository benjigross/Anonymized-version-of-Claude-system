# Draft Writing

## 1. Load voice and examples
- The voice guide (always). **Rule #1, "Say less," overrides everything.**
- The writing library INDEX, then examples for this format. Weight **Claude draft → my final**
  pairs most. Use pre-limit versions for voice, not length.

## 2. Load the substance
- `CONTEXT.md`; the tracker row, my doc section and recent Gmail for this firm or person
  (don't contradict what I've already told them).
- For firm-specific pieces: their thesis, recent investments and writing (research and cite).

## 3. Draft
- **Choose the points first:** list candidates, keep the 1–3 that matter most to this reader.
- Lead with my point of view, use concrete specifics, tie to the firm once, don't oversell.
- Limits are ceilings. Count with a tool. If over, cut whole points first, and offer cut options
  with word savings so I choose.

## 4. Deliver
- The draft; one line "Left out: …"; anything unverified.
- When I edit it, record new patterns in the voice guide (with scope). Save finals to the library.

## Rules
Never send or submit. Never put confidential interview numbers in third-party documents.
