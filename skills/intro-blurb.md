# Intro Blurb
1. Gather: the connector, the target (VC Firm 1 / Startup 1), the role link, and what's already been
   said (Gmail, tracker, my docs).
2. Pick one angle (voice guide, Positioning).
3. Write (A) the note to the connector: thanks, role link, resume attached, one background line,
   "see below for a blurb"; and (B) a 2–4 sentence third-person blurb in the connector's voice.
   Text-message variant if the offer came by text.
4. Draft only. After I send it, log it and save it to the writing library.
